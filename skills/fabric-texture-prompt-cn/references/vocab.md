# 质感词汇库

按质感五层分类。英文是实际写进提示词的，中文是意思。

## 1 面料本体

丝绸类
- heavy liquid silk satin 厚重如液体的缎面丝绸
- silk charmeuse 真丝双绉缎（垂坠、单面亮）
- silk taffeta 塔夫绸（挺括、有沙沙感）
- silk organza 欧根纱（透、挺）
- silk chiffon 雪纺（透、软、飘）
- silk georgette 乔其纱（透、有细绉）
- embroidered brocade 织锦缎
- jacquard silk with woven cloud patterns 提花丝绸（暗纹）
- xiangyunsha / gambiered Canton silk 香云纱（哑光、深色）

其他
- velvet with a deep plush pile 丝绒
- crushed velvet 压皱丝绒
- matte wool crepe 哑光羊毛绉
- cashmere knit with fine visible stitches 羊绒针织
- crisp cotton poplin 挺括棉府绸
- washed linen with natural slubs 水洗亚麻
- buttery lambskin leather 柔软小羊皮
- raw selvedge denim 原色赤耳牛仔
- tulle 网纱，lace 蕾丝（Chantilly lace 尚蒂伊蕾丝、guipure lace 立体蕾丝）

光泽
- soft pearly sheen 柔和珍珠光泽
- liquid sheen 液体般的光
- subtle luster 微光
- matte finish 哑光
- iridescent shimmer 幻彩
- 少用 glossy、shiny，容易塑料感

## 2 层次

- layered over 罩在……上
- multiple layers of translucent … 多层半透明
- a sheer overlay drifting over … 薄纱外罩
- lined with … 内衬……
- contrasting inner layer peeking at the collar and cuffs 领口袖口露出的撞色内层

## 3 立体工艺

- three-dimensional embroidery / raised embroidery 立体刺绣
- Suzhou silk embroidery 苏绣，satin stitch 缎面绣
- metallic thread embroidery / goldwork 金线绣
- couched gold cord 钉金绣
- sculptural floral appliqués 雕塑感花饰贴片
- hand-beaded with seed pearls and crystals 手工钉珠
- sequins catching the light 亮片
- hand-knotted frog buttons 手工盘扣
- piping along the edges 滚边
- pleats / knife pleats 褶裥 / 刀褶

分布写法
- densest at the collar, shoulders and hem, fading gently toward the waist
- scattered sparsely across the skirt
- a single large peony motif across the chest

## 4 动态

飘动
- almost weightless, billowing dramatically in the wind
- sweeping across the entire frame
- cascading ribbons
- caught mid-motion

褶皱
- sculptural curves 雕塑般的曲线
- intricate organic folds 复杂自然的褶皱
- soft drapery pooling on the floor 垂到地上堆叠
- crisp structured folds 挺括的折痕

修身
- fitted silhouette that follows her natural figure
- realistic satin highlights
- fine fabric tension lines along the body
- natural creases at the elbows and waist

## 5 光和材质

- realistic textile reflections 真实的织物反光
- light-catching gold threads 金线反光
- light glowing through sheer silk 光透过薄纱
- soft subsurface scattering through the sheer fabric 薄纱的次表面散射
- delicate rim lighting 轮廓光
- soft directional sunlight / window light 方向性柔光
- backlit translucent fabric 逆光下的透光面料

## 镜头

- macro-level textile detail 微距级面料细节
- shallow depth of field 浅景深
- low-angle cinematic composition 低机位电影感
- fabric occupying the entire frame 布料占满画面
- subtle film grain 轻微胶片颗粒

## 色板示例

- 青花：sapphire blue, porcelain white, antique ivory, powder blue, champagne gold
- 胭脂：rouge pink, pearl white, dusty rose, antique gold
- 墨绿：deep jade green, ink black, celadon, brushed bronze
- 月白：moonlight white, pale silver, mist grey, soft pearl
- 绛紫：plum purple, lilac grey, ivory, rose gold
