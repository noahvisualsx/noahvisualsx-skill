# 常见服装写法

每种写：版型关键词（必须写，防止被画成别的衣服）、适合的面料、动态放在哪、容易翻车的地方。

## 旗袍 qipao
- 版型：high mandarin collar, hand-knotted frog buttons, fitted silhouette, ankle-length hem, modest side slit at the calf
- 面料：heavy liquid silk satin、silk charmeuse、jacquard silk、velvet（冬季款）
- 动态：衣服不飘，交给披肩 / 披帛 / 拖尾；衣服写 tension lines 和 satin highlights
- 发型搭配：1930s Shanghai low chignon / finger waves
- 翻车：留了 hanfu、sleeves 会变汉服；开衩不写会被画成高开衩

## 汉服 hanfu
- 版型：cross-collar wrap (jiaoling), wide flowing sleeves, high-waisted skirt (ruqun) 或 floor-length robe
- 面料：silk chiffon、organza、brocade、embroidered satin
- 动态：袖子和裙摆自己飘，直接写 billowing
- 翻车：朝代不写会混搭；想要明制写 Ming-style mamianqun（马面裙）、standing collar

## 马面裙 mamianqun
- 版型：Ming-dynasty pleated horse-face skirt, flat front and back panels, knife pleats on the sides, gold woven hem band (lanqun)
- 面料：brocade with woven gold patterns、satin
- 动态：褶裥随步伐展开，写 pleats fanning out mid-stride
- 翻车：不写 flat panels 会变普通百褶裙

## 婚纱 / 高定礼服 gown
- 版型：structured corset bodice, sweetheart neckline 或 high neck, cathedral-length train
- 面料：silk mikado、tulle、Chantilly lace、organza
- 动态：拖尾和头纱飘
- 翻车：薄纱罩在身上容易透，写清 lined bodice

## 西装 tailored suit
- 版型：sharp tailored shoulders, peak lapels, single-breasted, high-waisted wide-leg trousers
- 面料：matte wool crepe、Super 150s wool、silk lining
- 动态：几乎不动，写 crisp structured folds、lining flashing at the hem；要动感就加一条丝巾或风衣
- 翻车：写 luxurious silk 会变睡衣质感

## 针织 knitwear
- 版型：fine-gauge rib knit cardigan / turtleneck
- 面料：cashmere、merino、mohair（毛茸）
- 动态：不飘，写 soft drape、visible stitches
- 翻车：不写 visible stitches 会像平滑的弹力布

## 皮革 leather
- 版型：cropped biker jacket / long trench
- 面料：buttery lambskin、patent leather（漆皮，慎用，易塑料）
- 动态：写 natural creases at the elbows、subtle wear
- 翻车：glossy 一多就是塑料，写 soft matte sheen

## 牛仔 denim
- 版型：high-rise straight leg, raw hem
- 面料：raw selvedge denim、washed denim with whiskering
- 动态：不飘，写 stiff structured folds、contrast stitching
